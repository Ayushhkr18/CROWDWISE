About CrowdWise

CrowdWise is a front-end Customer Experience Design capstone application engineered to help visitors navigate grand Indian festivals safely and calmly.

Demo Notice: All crowd values, zone density parameters, and route maps use generic sample data for evaluation and design demonstration.

Privacy Architecture

All sensitive family cards, offline QR passes, and preferences are strictly processed and stored locally within your browser's private storage (localStorage). No personal credentials, IDs, or home addresses are ever transmitted across networks or external servers.
